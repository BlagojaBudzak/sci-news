# Sci News — Physics — 2026-09-01

## Quantum Fourier Transform Toolbox: New Methods for Efficient Quantum Circuits

Researchers have developed two new approaches to constructing quantum Fourier transforms (QFTs), essential primitives in quantum algorithms. Using these methods, they have shown exponential improvements in circuit cost for specific group families, including the QFT over GL_2(F_q) and wreath products F ∙ S_n. These new tools provide a systematic way to construct QFTs for broad classes of finite groups, which is crucial for the development of efficient quantum algorithms.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28573v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28573v1)

## Long-Range Tails Reshape Non-Hermitian Spectra

In non-Hermitian systems, researchers have found that infinitesimal long-range hoppings can dramatically alter the spectrum and eigenstates of a short-range non-Hermitian system. This phenomenon is controlled by a competition between the decay length of long-range hoppings and the localization length of non-Hermitian skin modes. In one dimension, a squeezed generalized Brillouin zone replaces the original GBZ, while in two or higher dimensions, a squeezed amoeba formulation describes the reconstructed spectral density.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28577v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28577v1)

## Exact Quantification of Nonlocal Magic

Researchers have solved a long-standing problem in quantum mechanics by exactly quantifying nonlocal magic, a resource that lifts Clifford circuits to universal quantum computation. They found that the nonlocal magic of every pure multiqubit state is the distance of its entanglement spectrum from the closest spectrum of Bell pairs. This result has important implications for our understanding of quantum correlations and can be directly accessed through entanglement spectroscopy.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28563v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28563v1)

## Learning to Decode Concatenated Quantum Codes with Hierarchical Message Passing

Researchers have introduced a neural message-passing framework for decoding general concatenated stabilizer codes. This framework achieves substantially higher thresholds than the state-of-the-art bidirectional hard-decision decoder under both bit-flip and depolarizing noise. The resulting decoder can also achieve lower logical-CNOT failure rates than their dedicated decoder for many-hypercube codes.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28571v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28571v1)

## Switchable Chiral Antiferromagnetism through Nonlinear Magnetic Susceptibility

Researchers have generalized a scenario where the time-reversal partners of antiferromagnetic states can be selected deterministically through nonlinear magnetic susceptibility χ^(1). They applied this approach to a broad class of chiral antiferromagnets with zero net magnetization but field-switchable ground states. The origin of χ^(1) in these systems is illustrated using a 3-sublattice toy model, which also predicts a nontrivial temperature dependence of χ^(1).

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28546v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28546v1)
