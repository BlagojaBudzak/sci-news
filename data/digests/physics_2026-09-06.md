# Sci News — Physics — 2026-09-06

## Quantum Communication Limitations

Researchers have found that quantum communication and Bell nonlocality require an infinite amount of classical communication to simulate. This means that no matter how advanced the technology, it's impossible to perfectly replicate quantum systems using classical means. The team has resolved this problem for all quantum dimensions, revealing an unexpected transition at dimension four: no finite amount of classical communication can exactly simulate ququart communication or all quantum correlations of two entangled ququarts.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04182v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04182v1)

## Mechanical Networks Have Limitations

A team of researchers has discovered that tunable mechanical networks, which are being developed as materials that learn in place, have limitations. They found that when p degrees of freedom are both driven and read out, every reachable response block lies in a subspace of codimension p(p-1)/2. This means that nearly half the target space is unreachable, and any learning rule leaves an error at least the norm of the target's antisymmetric part on the shared degrees of freedom.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04169v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04169v1)

## Efficient Conversion of Optical to Mechanical States

Researchers have demonstrated efficient low-noise photon-phonon state transfer close to the single-quantum regime in an optomechanical crystal operated at Millikelvin temperatures. They achieved a record-level photon-phonon conversion efficiency of η=0.76, a mechanical storage lifetime of T_\mathrm{1}=7.3~μs, and a tunable conversion bandwidth exceeding 4.5 MHz using weak coherent optical input pulses at the few-photon level.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04150v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04150v1)

## Quantum Thermalization Achieves Optimal Approximate Quantum Error Correction

A team of researchers has leveraged the connection between quantum thermalization and quantum error correction to port the rigorous framework of (approximate) quantum error correction to the study of quantum thermalization. They found that treating typical late-time states as codewords, they can characterize the error-correcting properties of generic thermalizing dynamics. They uncovered a universal relationship between the encoding rate, distance, and thermal entropy density of the emergent code.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04121v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04121v1)

## Spurious Quantum Correlations

Researchers have discussed the existence of spurious quantum correlations, which are correlations that look quantum in one causal structure but have a natural classical explanation in another. They found that there are causal structures that admit non-classical quantum correlations, but for which the same correlations have a classical explanation in another causal structure without fine tuning.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04157v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04157v1)
