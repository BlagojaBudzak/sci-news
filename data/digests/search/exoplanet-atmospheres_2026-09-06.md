# Sci News — Exoplanet-Atmospheres — 2026-09-06

## New Mission Concept to Study Exoplanet Atmospheres

Researchers have proposed a new sounding rocket mission concept called CRAFTS (Coronal Radiation of Active F-Type Stars) to study the extreme ultraviolet radiation environments of nearby, active F-type stars. These stars are of interest because their EUV emission drives exoplanet atmospheric escape. The CRAFTS mission aims to determine if F-type stars are suitable targets for the Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO). The telescope design includes two off-axis parabolic mirrors and custom multi-layer coatings to achieve high EUV sensitivity.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3105456](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3105456)

## Trained Model Weights for Exoplanet Atmosphere Modeling

Researchers have made available the trained model weights and dataset-provenance metadata for their paper 'Accelerating Chemical Kinetics for Exoplanet Atmospheres using Neural Networks'. The model weights and metadata are provided for the PyTorch Lightning checkpoint, the exported deployable model, and the training metrics/logs. The emulator code is available on GitHub, and the Robertson benchmark code is also available on GitHub.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21865963](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21865963)

## Upgraded Framework for Exoplanet Atmosphere Modeling

Researchers have released an updated version of the Pyrat Bay modeling framework, version 2.0, tailored for the characterization of exoplanet atmospheres with the observational capabilities of current facilities, such as the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST). The upgraded framework includes a standalone chemistry package, chemcat, and other key implementations, such as nested sampling via MultiNest and isotopic-ratio fitting. The researchers validated the thermochemical- and radiative-equilibrium modules via comparisons with other codes.

**Read the paper:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07128](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07128)

## Future Perspectives on Exoplanet Atmosphere Studies

Researchers have discussed the future perspectives on studying exoplanet atmospheres, highlighting the advancements made over the last two decades. They noted that exoplanet characterization has advanced from mere detection of atmospheres to quantifying their detailed composition and 3D atmospheric dynamics. The researchers emphasized the importance of innovative instrumentation and observational strategies, using time-, spectral-, and spatial-resolution to reveal worlds from a single pixel.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21841551](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21841551)
