# Sci News — Ai-Assisted-Drug-Discovery — 2026-09-07

## AI Helps Identify New Cancer Treatments

Researchers have developed an AI-driven drug discovery framework to identify potential inhibitors for head and neck squamous cell carcinoma. The framework combines graph neural networks and structure-based simulations to analyze molecular graphs and predict the effectiveness of potential inhibitors. Using curated bioactivity datasets, the team trained seven predictive models and constructed a consensus ensemble framework that achieved high precision and recall on independent validation datasets. The framework identified 36,771 and 391 high-confidence hits in the ChEMBL and DrugBank libraries, respectively, with significant overlap with DrugBank.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41698-026-01661-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41698-026-01661-1)

## AI in Drug Repurposing: A Review

This review provides an overview of AI-driven drug repurposing, a strategy that identifies new therapeutic indications for existing approved or investigational drugs. The review covers computational strategies, publicly available biomedical databases, feature representation methods, machine learning, and deep learning architectures. It discusses how AI has transformed computational drug repurposing into a data-driven and efficient discipline, enabling the integration of heterogeneous datasets and improving prediction accuracy.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.36948/ijfmr.2026.v08i04.85535](https://doi.org/10.36948/ijfmr.2026.v08i04.85535)

## AI Optimizes Drug Delivery Systems

Researchers have explored the use of AI to optimize drug delivery systems, which are essential for modern medicine. AI technologies, including machine learning and deep learning, have made predictive modeling, formulation optimization, and tailored therapy possible. The study examines how AI-assisted optimization affects therapeutic results in drug delivery systems, including advanced drug carrier design, pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic behavior prediction, and real-time drug release monitoring.

## AI-Driven Drug Repurposing Methodology

This review focuses on an AI-driven drug repurposing methodology for selecting lead combinations of drugs for various diseases. The methodology encompasses disease characterization, integration of genomic and multi-omics data, identification of novel biomolecular targets, prediction of drug-target interactions, and prioritization of existing drugs for potential repurposing. The review emphasizes the importance of AI-based prediction of drug synergism and antagonism, where molecular targets, biological pathways, and drug-response profiles are integrated to identify promising combinations.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.66204/gjpsr-1442-2026-2-9-5](https://doi.org/10.66204/gjpsr-1442-2026-2-9-5)

## AI in Infectious Disease Drug Development

This review explores the transformative role of AI and machine learning in addressing challenges in drug discovery and development for infectious diseases. AI models and machine learning algorithms excel at integrating and analyzing diverse datasets, including genomic, proteomic, chemical, clinical, and epidemiological data, to accelerate target identification, optimize compound screening, and predict drug efficacy and safety. The review synthesizes recent advances, highlighting the application of supervised and deep learning models in pathogen identification, antimicrobial susceptibility testing, and the rational design of novel therapeutics.
