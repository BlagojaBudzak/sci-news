## Unlocking Sodium-Ion Batteries' Potential

Researchers have discovered a new mechanism to enhance the stability of sodium-ion batteries. They found that adding a specific type of aromatic additive to the battery's anode can create a protective layer that prevents the battery from degrading over time. This layer, called a solid electrolyte interphase, is crucial for maintaining the battery's performance and safety. The team's findings could lead to the development of more efficient and reliable sodium-ion batteries.

## Designing Better Anodes for Sodium-Ion Batteries

Scientists have developed a new way to design the surface of hard carbon anodes in sodium-ion batteries. By creating a specific pattern of chemical groups on the surface, they can control the flow of ions and prevent the battery from degrading. This approach leads to improved performance and stability, with the battery retaining 96.5% of its capacity after 5,000 cycles.
