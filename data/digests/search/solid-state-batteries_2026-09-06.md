# Sci News — Solid-State-Batteries — 2026-09-06

## High-Voltage Solid-State Batteries That Work in Extreme Temperatures

Researchers have developed a new type of solid-state battery that can operate at high voltages and extreme temperatures, from -60°C to 55°C. The battery uses a dual-phase halide solid electrolyte that combines the stability of a crystalline phase with the conductivity of an amorphous matrix. This innovative design enables the battery to retain 90.5% of its capacity after 100 cycles at 4.8V and maintain a specific capacity of 133 mA h g-1 over 500 cycles at 55°C and 3C.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.4852008](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.4852008)

## A New Class of Solid-State Batteries That Store Energy in Electric Fields

Scientists have introduced a new class of solid-state batteries that store energy in structured electric fields rather than chemical bonds. This approach eliminates the need for lithium or faradaic chemistry and enables fast charging, high-density energy storage, and scalability. The researchers have developed three engineered architectures for these batteries, including doped semiconductor parallel plate cells, multilayer PCB-style stacked capacitive banks, and hybrid N/P doped dielectric fusion cells.

## Improving Solid-State Batteries with Tribochemical Interface Engineering

Researchers have investigated the use of amorphous titanium tetrasulfide (TiS4) as a high-capacity cathode material for sulfide-based solid-state batteries. They found that high-energy ball milling (HBM) processing intensity determines the composite microstructure and electrochemical performance. HBM-processed composites exhibit superior homogenization and achieve discharge capacities of 1110 mAh g S-1 at 0.05C and 669 mAh g S-1 at 0.5C.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.1002/batt.70459](https://doi.org/10.1002/batt.70459)

## High-Entropy Design for Improved Ionic Conductivity and Air Stability

Scientists have developed a high-entropy design strategy to improve the ionic conductivity and air stability of lithium argyrodites. By substituting the P-site in Li6PS5I with Ge, Sb, Si, and As, they created a material with a configurational entropy of 2.04R. The resulting material exhibits room-temperature ionic conductivities of 8.0 mS cm-1 in a cold-pressed state and 11.4 mS cm-1 for hot-pressed pellets.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.1002/smtd.70963](https://doi.org/10.1002/smtd.70963)

## Accurate State Estimation for Solid-State Batteries

Researchers have proposed a Dual-Extended Kalman Filter (DEKF) framework for joint estimation of state of charge (SOC) and state of temperature (SOT) in solid-state lithium-ion batteries. They established a second-order equivalent circuit model and a two-state thermal model, and designed a DEKF-based estimator to simultaneously estimate SOC, surface temperature, and core temperature. Validation under four typical dynamic driving cycles showed accurate SOC and temperature estimation.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.1002/ente.70635](https://doi.org/10.1002/ente.70635)
