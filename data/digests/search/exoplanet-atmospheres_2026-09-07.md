# Sci News — Exoplanet-Atmospheres — 2026-09-07

## Unlocking the Secrets of Venus' Atmosphere

Researchers have found that the DAVINCI mission will provide the high-fidelity atmospheric profile data needed to resolve degeneracies in terrestrial exoplanet spectra. These degeneracies occur when atmospheric composition, cloud structure, and surface conditions are difficult to distinguish. The team used the Venus benchmark to demonstrate how atmospheric profile uncertainties translate into uncertainties in modeled transmission and thermal emission spectra of CO2-dominated terrestrial worlds. They showed that the spread in the modeled Venus benchmark spectrum will decrease by factors of 4-5 for transmission and 5-15 times for thermal emission after DAVINCI.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00147v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00147v1)

## Chemical Tracers for 3D Atmospheric Asymmetries on WASP-69 b

Warm giant exoplanets like WASP-69b have strong three-dimensional temperature contrasts that can significantly alter atmospheric chemistry. Researchers used a three-dimensional general circulation model to investigate the relative influence of atmospheric composition and thermal structure on atmospheric chemistry and transmission spectra. They found that CH4 exhibits strong latitudinal variations linked to deep quench temperatures, while SO2 shows longitudinal asymmetries driven by upper-atmospheric photochemistry and irradiation geometry. CO2 remains comparatively insensitive to spatial thermal variations and emerges as a robust tracer of atmospheric metallicity.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11881v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11881v1)

## A New Space Telescope for Precision Astrophysical Observations

A 3.5-meter segmented-mirror robotic space telescope is being proposed for precision astrophysical observations and rapid-response transient astronomy. The telescope will have a Cassegrain optical configuration optimized to deliver diffraction-limited performance across a wide, flat focal plane. The proposed scientific payload includes a Wide-field Camera, a spectroscopic instrument, and an optional Exoplanet Imaging Coronagraph. Planned investigations include gravitational-wave counterparts, rapidly evolving transients, Type Ia supernova cosmology, direct imaging of exoplanets, and exoplanet atmospheric spectroscopy.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02571v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02571v1)

## Detecting Hydrocarbons in Titan's Atmosphere

Researchers used high-resolution cross-correlation spectroscopy (HRCCS) to detect hydrocarbons in Titan's atmosphere. They developed a new cross-section-based methodology for HRCCS template construction and analyzed K-band CRIRES+ observations of Titan. The analysis recovered known hydrocarbons such as methane and acetylene, and yielded the first HRCCS detection of ethane with a significance of SNRpeak = 5.17 ± 0.07. The ethane detection was made possible exclusively through cross-section-based templates, as no high-resolution line list currently exists for this molecule.

**Read the paper:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03975v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03975v1)
