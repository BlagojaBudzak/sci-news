## Hybrid AI Model for Early Detection of Structural Irregularities in Earthquake-Resistant Architectural Design

Researchers developed a hybrid AI-driven framework for detecting structural irregularities in earthquake-resistant architectural design. The system, called Irregularity Detector (ID.1), uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to analyze architectural plan images and combines this with analytical structural parameters. The model was trained on 1,576 architectural plan samples and achieved a validation accuracy of 99.2% and an F1-score of 0.98.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.13189/cea.2026.140508](https://doi.org/10.13189/cea.2026.140508)

## Seismic vulnerability assessment of construction projects: A multi-criteria approach

Researchers applied the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) to evaluate seismic vulnerability in construction projects. They used a structured multi-criteria decision-making model to assess key parameters, including human resources, materials, supply logistics, and organizational practices. The study found that human and organizational factors significantly influence overall vulnerability.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.5459/bnzsee.1776](https://doi.org/10.5459/bnzsee.1776)

## Analisis perilaku sambungan replaceable dengan mekanisme kuncian dan sambungan konvensional pada Sistem Rangka Baja Pemikul Momen Khusus

Researchers developed a replaceable connection with a locking mechanism for the Special Moment-Resisting Frame (SMRF) system. They evaluated the connection's performance using numerical analysis and found that it achieved a maximum capacity of 1.015,7 kN, which is 92.94% higher than the conventional connection.
