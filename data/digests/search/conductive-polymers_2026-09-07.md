# Sci News — Conductive-Polymers — 2026-09-07

## Dicationic Ion-Pair Doping Enables Stable and High-Performing Conducting Polymers for Organic Thermoelectrics

Researchers have developed a new doping method for conducting polymers, which enables stable and high-performing thermoelectric materials. They used a dicationic ion-pair dopant, TAB-2TFSI, to improve the electrical conductivity and power factor of poly(3-hexylthiophene-2,5-diyl) (P3HT). The results show that TAB-2TFSI delivers the highest average electrical conductivity of 290 ± 30 S cm-1 and power factor of 40 ± 6 µW m-1 K-2, making P3HT one of the top high-performing isotropic doped polymer thermoelectrics ever reported.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77036](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.77036)

## Polyaniline as a Conductive Polymer and Its Role in Improving the Efficiency and Conductivity of Perovskite Solar Cells

Researchers have investigated the role of polyaniline as a conductive polymer in perovskite solar cells. They found that incorporating polyaniline into the transport layers improves the efficiency and conductivity of the cells. The results show that power conversion efficiency increased by 17.5% with graphene oxide and by 36.8% with reduced graphene oxide, and short-circuit current density improved by 9.8% and 23.1%, respectively.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.24924](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2608.24924)

## The Effect of Polypyrrole Concentration Prepared by Electrospinning Nanofibers on NH3 Gas Sensing Performance

Researchers have investigated the effect of polypyrrole concentration on the gas sensing performance of electrospun nanofibers. They found that the optimal polypyrrole concentration is 0.05 wt%, which provides the greatest sensitivity and fastest response and relaxation times. The results show that the fibers presented reversible and repeatable chemiresistive cycles characteristic of p-type materials.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.29072/vrp9dh05](https://doi.org/10.29072/vrp9dh05)

## Stabilization of Negative Capacitance by a Copper-Based Polymer and Sodium Polyacrylate-Derived Conductive Polymer Hydrogel for Rapid Charge-Discharge Supercapacitor

Researchers have developed a new material for rapid charge-discharge supercapacitors, which stabilizes negative capacitance. They used a copper-based polymer and sodium polyacrylate-derived conductive polymer hydrogel to improve the capacitance and stability of the material. The results show that the hybrid maintains nearly constant capacitance for up to 96 h under ambient conditions without encapsulation.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.1021/acsapm.6c02673](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsapm.6c02673)

## Detection, Reconstruction, and Overheating Warning of Three-Dimensional Dynamic Temperature Fields Inside Conductive Polymer Gels

Researchers have developed a new method for detecting and reconstructing three-dimensional dynamic temperature fields inside conductive polymer gels. They used a focused light-field infrared camera to acquire infrared light-field images and reconstruct the temperature field. The results show that the method can detect overheating and provide early warning signals.

**Read the paper:** [https://doi.org/10.3390/polym18172141](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym18172141)
